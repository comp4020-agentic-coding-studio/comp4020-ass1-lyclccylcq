About this book

Learn Go by playing, not reading.

This is a short, interactive rulebook for absolute beginners. Each chapter teaches exactly one rule of Go on a real 9×9 board — you place the stones yourself and watch the rule happen, rather than reading a description of it.

Interaction is the point: every chapter is a small puzzle to play through, not a page to skim. By the end you will have placed a stone, captured a group, met an illegal move, seen a repeating position forbidden by ko, and scored a finished game — the whole shape of the game, one rule at a time.

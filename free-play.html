Free play

The chapters used a small 9×9 board because it fits on a page. This is the real one: nineteen lines each way, 361 intersections, the board the game is actually played on.

Your opponent is a simple practice bot living in this page. It knows the rules and it will take a stone it can take, rescue a group it can rescue, and put yours in danger when it sees the chance — but it is not strong, and it is not meant to be. It is something to play against while the rules settle.

Pass when you have nothing worth playing. Two passes in a row end the game, exactly as in chapter six.

1. Placing stones on intersections

Go is played on the crossing points of a grid, not inside its squares. A stone always sits on an intersection.

Try it: click any empty point on the board to place a stone. Players alternate colours, starting with black, exactly as they would across the table.

This board doesn't know the rest of the rules yet — liberties, captures, and legality come in later chapters. For now it only answers one question: where do stones go?

Go begins with one simple action: place a stone on an intersection. But once a stone is placed, what keeps it alive?

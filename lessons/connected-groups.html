3. Connected groups

Stones of the same colour that touch orthogonally — sharing an edge, not just a corner — form a single group. A group shares its liberties, and it lives or dies together.

Work through three short boards, each with its own black group. For each one: click a stone to see the whole group and its shared liberties, then place white stones yourself until nothing is left.

Connected stones share their liberties and live or die together. If liberties decide whether stones live, can you place a stone where it has none?

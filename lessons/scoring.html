7. Winning the Game

Both players have passed, so the board in front of you is final. Nothing more will be played on it — which leaves one question outstanding, and it is the only one the rules haven't answered yet: who won?

Go is scored by area: each player's score is their own stones on the board, plus every empty point that only their stones surround. Whoever ends with the higher score wins. White also receives a fixed bonus called komi, added to make up for Black playing first.

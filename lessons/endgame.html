6. Endgame

Games don't end suddenly. Long before the last stone, the big fights are over and both players are settling boundaries: closing their own gaps, and taking the points that still belong to nobody. This is the endgame, 收官.

The position below is one of those. Nothing is in danger and nothing is in doubt — but three points are still worth playing. Find them one at a time. A stone somewhere safe and settled is legal and completely useless, so the board will tell you when you've played one.

When neither player can gain anything more, they stop. Saying so is a move of its own: you pass. Two passes in a row, and the game is over.

The stones have stopped moving. Now: who actually won?

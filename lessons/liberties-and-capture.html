2. Liberties and capture

A stone survives only as long as it has liberties — empty points touching it. The black stone below has four.

Place a white stone on each highlighted point to take a liberty away. When the last one is gone, the black stone is captured and removed from the board.

Every stone needs open neighbouring intersections — liberties. A stone does not always stand alone.

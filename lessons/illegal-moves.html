4. Illegal moves: suicide

A stone can't be played if it (or its whole group) would end up with zero liberties — unless the move captures an opponent group first, which frees up liberties of its own.

Two boards below both have one empty point that looks completely surrounded. Try playing Black there in each and see what actually happens.

A move with no resulting liberties is illegal — unless the move captures first. That's every rule this book covers: where stones go, what keeps them alive, how groups share liberties, and when a move is illegal.
